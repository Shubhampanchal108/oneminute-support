## ✨ Overview

One Minute Support is a B2B SaaS platform that enables businesses to create and deploy AI-powered customer support chatbots without writing complex AI infrastructure.

The platform automatically learns from your website content, uploaded documents, and custom knowledge sources to provide accurate and context-aware responses to visitors.

---

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Businesses often struggle with:

- Repetitive customer queries
- Limited support availability
- High customer support costs
- Slow response times
- Lost leads outside business hours

One Minute Support solves these problems by providing an intelligent AI chatbot that works 24/7 and is trained specifically on your business information.

## ⚡ How It Works

### 1. Add Your Website

Enter your website URL.

```text
https://yourcompany.com
```

The platform automatically crawls and extracts relevant content.

### 2. Upload Knowledge Sources

Train your chatbot using:

- Website pages
- PDF documents
- DOCX files
- Text files
- FAQs
- Custom written content

### 3. Create AI Knowledge Base

The system:

- Processes content
- Chunks information
- Generates embeddings
- Stores data in a vector database
- Creates a searchable AI knowledge base

### 4. Generate Script

Receive a simple script tag:

```html
<script
  src="https://oneminutesupport.com/widget.js"
  data-bot-id="YOUR_BOT_ID">
</script>
```

### 5. Deploy Anywhere

Paste the script into your website and your AI chatbot goes live instantly.
